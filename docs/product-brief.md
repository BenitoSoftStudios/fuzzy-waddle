# Product Brief — BuildQuote

## What It Is
BuildQuote is a contractor estimate generator. Contractors fill in their info, client info, and line items, then download a professional PDF estimate.

## The Problem
Contractors (plumbers, electricians, roofers, painters, general contractors) send estimates every day. Most use Word templates, Google Docs, or handwritten forms. These look unprofessional, take too long, and are hard to customize per job.

There are no simple, fast, focused tools for this. Existing tools (ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro) are expensive CRMs aimed at large operations. A solo contractor doesn't need a CRM — they need a clean PDF they can send to a client in 3 minutes.

## The Audience
- Independent contractors and small contractor businesses (1–10 employees)
- Trades: plumbing, electrical, roofing, HVAC, painting, landscaping, carpentry, general contracting
- They send 1–20 estimates per week
- They pay for tools that save them time and look professional

## The Solution
1. Fill out a simple form (your info, client info, line items, tax rate)
2. See a live preview of the estimate
3. Download a clean, professional PDF — free with watermark, $4.99 for clean version
